# Product

> A professional website that showcases engineering capability through projects, technical writing, and public engineering evidence.

---

# Overview

This website is the central hub of the Project HS brand.

Its purpose is to connect projects, technical articles, and engineering documentation into a single, coherent experience.

Rather than functioning as a traditional personal blog, the website should communicate engineering capability through public work.

---

# Product Vision

Visitors should understand three things within the first few moments:

- Who I am
- What I build
- Why my engineering work is trustworthy

Every page should reinforce these messages.

---

# Target Audience

Primary audience:

- AI Engineers
- Software Engineers
- Engineering Managers
- Technical Recruiters
- Future collaborators

Secondary audience:

- Students interested in AI Engineering
- Developers exploring production AI systems

---

# Product Goals

The website should:

- Showcase engineering projects.
- Publish technical articles.
- Explain engineering decisions.
- Document experiments and lessons learned.
- Demonstrate long-term engineering growth.

---

# Core Experiences

The product consists of four primary experiences.

## 1. Discover

Visitors quickly understand the engineer behind the work.

## 2. Explore

Visitors browse projects and technical articles.

## 3. Learn

Visitors gain practical engineering knowledge through well-documented content.

## 4. Trust

Visitors develop confidence in the engineering capability demonstrated by the published work.

---

# Information Architecture

The initial site structure should include:

- Home
- About
- Projects
- Articles
- Resume
- Contact

Additional sections may be introduced as the project evolves.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature should satisfy at least one of the following:

- Improves discoverability
- Improves learning
- Improves trust
- Improves readability

If it satisfies none of these, reconsider whether it belongs in the product.

---

# Non-Goals

This website is not intended to become:

- A social media platform
- A personal journal
- A tutorial repository for beginners
- A marketing landing page

The focus remains on engineering evidence and technical communication.

---

# Evolution

This product will evolve incrementally.

New features should strengthen the core experience rather than increase complexity.

Growth should be intentional and sustainable.
