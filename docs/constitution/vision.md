# Vision

> Build a trusted AI engineering brand by creating public engineering evidence.

---

# Overview

This project exists to build a trusted professional identity as an AI engineer.

Rather than relying on resumes or claims, it aims to demonstrate engineering capability through public work that others can explore, understand, and learn from.

Every project, article, and technical document should contribute to a growing body of engineering evidence.

---

# Mission

Design, build, document, and continuously improve production-oriented AI systems while openly sharing the engineering decisions behind them.

The objective is not only to build software, but also to communicate the reasoning, trade-offs, and lessons learned throughout the engineering process.

---

# Vision

This website serves as the central hub for my engineering work.

It brings together projects, technical writing, and documentation to showcase practical AI engineering in a clear and structured way.

Over time, it will evolve alongside my career and become a long-term portfolio of engineering knowledge and experience.

---

# Guiding Principles

When making decisions, prefer:

- Systems over demos.
- Documentation is engineering.
- Engineering should leave evidence.
- Long-term value over short-term attention.
- Evidence over opinion.
- Identity is stable. Execution evolves.

---

# Success

Success is not defined solely by traffic or popularity.

Instead, success means consistently building and sharing engineering work that is:

- Practical
- Well documented
- Technically sound
- Valuable to other engineers
- Representative of continuous growth

---

# Non-Goals

This project is **not** intended to become:

- A personal diary
- A lifestyle blog
- A news aggregation site
- An AI hype platform

Every published artifact should provide meaningful engineering value.

---

# Evolution

This document is intentionally lightweight.

The vision should remain stable, while the details evolve as new projects, experiences, and insights are accumulated.

The website will grow over time, and this document should evolve with it without losing its original purpose.
